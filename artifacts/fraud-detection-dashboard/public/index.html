Questions. Answered.

Who's behind Vaultic Platform? +
Vaultic is engineered as a privacy-first federated learning platform specifically designed for enterprise financial networks. It allows banks to collaboratively train fraud prevention models without sharing raw customer data.
How does Federated Learning protect customer privacy? +
Raw transaction logs never leave the bank's local firewall. Training happens locally on bank servers, and only encrypted model weight updates (gradients) are transmitted to the global engine.
What is Differential Privacy ($\epsilon$-calibrated noise)? +
Differential Privacy adds mathematically calibrated Gaussian noise to model weights ($\epsilon=1.0$), ensuring that no individual customer or transaction can be reverse-engineered from global model parameters.
How does Secure Aggregation guarantee zero eavesdropping? +
Secure Aggregation uses zero-sum cryptographic masks ($\sum M_k - \sum R_k = \sum W_k$). When individual masks are added together at the central server, they perfectly cancel out ($\delta < 10^{-9}$), revealing only the aggregate model without exposing any single bank's raw weights.
